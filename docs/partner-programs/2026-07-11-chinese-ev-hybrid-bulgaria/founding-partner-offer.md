# AutoMarket founding importer pilot

**Status:** Internal, approval-ready proposal. It is not an offer accepted by any brand, importer, or distributor.

## Purpose

Give one verified Bulgarian importer or distributor a low-friction way to test AutoMarket as a stock-discovery and lead-routing channel. The pilot is non-exclusive, time-limited, and does not confer an endorsement in either direction.

## Proposed pilot

- One verified importer/distributor organization profile, subject to documentary verification and AutoMarket policy.
- Assisted onboarding for 20–100 current Bulgarian-stock vehicles through an agreed CSV or feed.
- One public brand/model collection linked to live inventory, not a promotional landing page with empty results.
- Test-drive and quote enquiries routed to an agreed dealer or central lead owner.
- Clear fields for official-import status, stock location, estimated delivery, warranty, service coverage, parts support, and finance assumptions.
- A weekly operational report covering inventory freshness, feed errors, views, saves, qualified enquiries, response time, and scheduled appointments.
- Bulgarian and English listing-content support within approved factual source material.

## Commercial posture

- Founding access may be free for a limited evaluation period; duration and any later price require written agreement.
- No exclusivity, guaranteed lead volume, guaranteed ranking, or implied manufacturer endorsement.
- Paid placement, if later offered, must be visibly labelled and must not change verification status.
- The partner remains responsible for vehicle availability, prices, warranty terms, delivery estimates, finance disclosures, and lead fulfilment.
- AutoMarket may pause inaccurate, stale, or unverifiable inventory.

## Value hypothesis

This is an inference to validate, not a fact about any target: an official local importer can reduce buyer uncertainty by publishing current stock and ownership-support facts in one comparable marketplace format. AutoMarket can reduce onboarding effort through a narrow feed contract and return structured demand signals without asking the partner to replace its existing website or dealer systems.

## Pilot exit criteria

At the end of the agreed evaluation period, both parties review data quality, operational burden, buyer engagement, lead quality, and response performance. Continuation, pricing, placement, and scope are decided explicitly; silence does not convert the pilot into a paid or exclusive arrangement.
