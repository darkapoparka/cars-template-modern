# Partner profiles and qualification

**Method:** Facts below come from the official sources linked in each profile and catalogued in [sources.md](sources.md). “Fit,” “readiness,” and recommended motion are AutoMarket inferences. All pages were accessed on 2026-07-11.

## 1. Leapmotor — SFA Automotive

**Readiness:** Tier A; first-wave priority.

**Authority confidence:** Very high.

### Sourced context

- Leapmotor/Stellantis announced on 2025-06-05 that SFA Automotive LTD had signed an agreement to introduce Leapmotor in Bulgaria, initially with T03 and C10 and a growing local sales/service network. [Official announcement](https://www.media.stellantis.com/em-en/leapmotor/press/leapmotor-accelerates-european-expansion-with-new-market-entries-and-dealer-appointments).
- SFA describes itself as the official Bulgarian importer for Leapmotor. Its group-wide network statement must not be presented as a Leapmotor-only network. [SFA company page](https://webstore.sfa.bg/page/za-nas).
- The current official locator shows Bulgarian Leapmotor sales/service routes, including Sofia, Ruse, and Dobrich, with additional current locations visible through the locator. [Leapmotor Bulgaria locator](https://sfa.bg/root/crm/Leapmotor/dealer/index.html).
- Local price lists document vehicle and traction-battery warranty terms by model; Leapmotor’s European service page describes Stellantis-network servicing and European genuine-parts logistics. [T03 price list](https://sfa.bg/root/crm/Leapmotor/Price-lists/Price-List-T03.pdf), [C10 price list](https://sfa.bg/root/crm/Leapmotor/Price-lists/Price-List-C10.pdf), [European service](https://www.leapmotor.net/service).

### Official contact path

Use SFA’s central switchboard, `02 892 1165`, or the official dealer/service locator and its published general sales route, `office@sofia4.sfa-retail.bg`. Ask for the importer’s digital inventory, commercial partnerships, or dealer-network owner. Do not route first contact through a named press officer.

### Qualification

- **Verified:** importer path, operating sales/service points, model-level warranty material, European parts-distribution route.
- **Needs confirmation:** central feed owner, per-unit stock and delivery, finance terms, lead-routing SLA, image rights.
- **Fit inference:** strongest first-wave candidate because authority and aftersales evidence are unusually clear.

## Standard qualification questions

Ask every candidate for:

1. Current legal importer/distributor entity, brand/model territory, and manufacturer authorization evidence.
2. Live Bulgarian stock by stable unit/configuration ID, physical location, demonstrator status, reservation state, and delivery window.
3. Warranty by vehicle, battery, and powertrain; transferability; roadside assistance; and which sites perform warranty work.
4. Parts warehouse and escalation route, typical availability, back-order handling, and replacement-mobility policy.
5. VAT-inclusive cash price and complete finance fields, including lender, deposit, term, APR/GPR, fees, final payment, total payable, eligibility, and validity.
6. Feed format, refresh cadence, error ownership, image rights, lead-routing SLA, consent/data-processing terms, and takedown process.
