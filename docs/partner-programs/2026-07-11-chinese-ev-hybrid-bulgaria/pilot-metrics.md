# Pilot measurement framework

## Measurement principle

Targets must be agreed with the selected partner after baseline stock, traffic, staffing, and attribution are known. The measures below define what to observe; they are not promised outcomes.

## Operational metrics

| Metric | Definition | Suggested cadence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inventory freshness | Share of live records updated within the agreed freshness window | Daily |
| Feed acceptance rate | Accepted records divided by submitted records | Per import and weekly |
| Material error rate | Records blocked by price, identity, availability, media, or compliance errors | Per import and weekly |
| Stale-stock rate | Public records beyond the agreed freshness threshold | Daily |
| Price consistency | Public AutoMarket price matching the partner source at comparison time | Daily sample |
| Availability consistency | Sampled stock state matching the partner source or confirmation | Daily sample |
| Lead delivery success | Leads acknowledged by the agreed destination without routing failure | Daily |
| Lead response time | Time from valid lead delivery to first human response | Weekly distribution |

## Funnel metrics

Report counts and rates with definitions held constant:

1. Collection and partner-profile impressions.
2. Vehicle-detail views from those surfaces.
3. Saves and saved-search demand.
4. Quote and test-drive submissions.
5. Valid leads after spam, duplicate, and invalid-contact exclusions.
6. Partner-accepted qualified leads using an agreed qualification rule.
7. Appointments scheduled.
8. Test drives completed, if the partner can return this event lawfully and reliably.

Do not infer sales from clicks or leads. Sale and revenue attribution require an agreed return event and attribution window.

## Trust and content quality

- Completeness of local stock, delivery, warranty, service, parts, and transparent-finance fields.
- Percentage of public claims carrying a source and last-verified date.
- Media-rights completeness and broken-image rate.
- Buyer corrections or complaints by category and resolution time.
- Records suppressed because evidence or freshness fell below policy.

## Review rhythm

- Launch week: daily feed-health and lead-routing check.
- Weekly: operational and funnel report with anomalies and corrective actions.
- Mid-pilot: review data burden, buyer questions, and whether qualification rules need adjustment.
- End of pilot: joint decision on continuation, scope, commercial terms, and measurement changes.

## Decision framework

Continue only if the pilot demonstrates acceptable data accuracy, sustainable partner workload, functioning lead operations, and useful buyer engagement. High traffic does not compensate for stale inventory, unclear ownership support, or unanswered enquiries.
