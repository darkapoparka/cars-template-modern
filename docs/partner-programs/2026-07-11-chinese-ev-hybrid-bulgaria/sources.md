## Evidence limitations

- Official sites can be stale, internally inconsistent, or technically unavailable. The XPENG privacy-page issue, VOYAH Bulgaria site failure during research, and NIO/firefly scope conflict are recorded rather than resolved by inference.
- Offer, configurator, and dealer pages change. Recheck authority, stock, price, warranty, delivery, and finance immediately before any external use.
- A general contact route establishes reachability, not permission to send bulk outreach or process personal data.
- This register records public official evidence only; it does not substitute for contracting, authorization, data-protection, or media-rights review.
