## Purpose

This packet turns current official-source evidence into a supply-acquisition program for AutoMarket. The first target is normally the organization that controls Bulgarian stock, pricing, warranty, service, parts, delivery, and lead routing—not a generic manufacturer inbox.

The terms “target,” “route,” and “candidate” describe research findings. They do not mean that AutoMarket has an official, commercial, or endorsed relationship with any named organization.

## Market rationale

ACEA’s provisional January–May 2026 figures show Bulgarian registrations growing year over year for battery-electric vehicles (+87.2%), plug-in hybrids (+63.0%), and hybrid-electric vehicles (+103.8%), albeit from a relatively small base. That supports testing a mixed EV/hybrid acquisition program, not claiming assured demand. The same evidence strengthens the case for accurate availability and ownership-support information because fast category growth does not establish stock, service, or affordability by itself. See the [ACEA May 2026 release](https://www.acea.auto/files/Press_release_car_registrations_May_2026.pdf).

The European Alternative Fuels Observatory reports that Bulgaria’s total EV stock grew 53.5% in 2024 while identifying affordability, infrastructure, and incentive constraints. AutoMarket should therefore compete on verified official import, local stock, warranty, service, parts, delivery, and transparent finance rather than origin-based promotion. See the [EAFO Bulgaria update](https://alternative-fuels-observatory.ec.europa.eu/general-information/news/bulgaria-total-ev-stock-grew-535-2024).

## Non-negotiable claims policy

- “Official importer,” “official distributor,” or “official representative” requires a current manufacturer-controlled source or equivalent official local legal evidence.
- “Verified” requires stored evidence, reviewer, scope, and review date; paid placement never creates verification.
- Stock and delivery are vehicle-level, time-sensitive facts. A model page or configurator is not proof of a particular unit’s availability.
- Warranty, battery coverage, roadside assistance, and service terms remain model-, version-, seller-, and date-specific.
- Finance display requires provider, cash price, deposit, term, monthly payment, interest/APR or Bulgarian GPR where applicable, fees, final payment, total payable, eligibility, and validity date.
- Absence of public evidence is a diligence gap, not proof that a capability does not exist.
