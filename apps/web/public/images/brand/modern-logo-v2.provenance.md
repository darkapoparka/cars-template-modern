Initial generation prompt: One finished premium automotive brand logo reading
exactly MODERN, uppercase, on a genuinely transparent background. A compact
angular M emblem on the left and a crisp custom geometric sans-serif wordmark
on the right. Bold enough for a 144px mobile header, balanced spacing, solid
dark charcoal, flat vector-like shapes. No tagline, badge, scene, shadows,
bevels, chrome texture, decorative background or checkerboard.

Delivery removes transparent margins and isolated nearly invisible alpha noise
outside the visible artwork. It preserves the cropped image at its original
1843x264 resolution in lossless WebP (148,104 bytes). Every retained alpha pixel
and visible RGB pixel matches the approved PNG; there is no resize, tracing,
replacement font or SVG conversion. Source and derivative hashes are recorded
in `provenance/assets/modern-logo-v2/generation.json`.

The shared logo component uses this image's alpha as a CSS mask to select white
or dark foreground color without changing the generated lettering or emblem.
Configuration remains `desktopPreviewIdentity.markArtwork`, restricted to the
static template master by the existing source-slug guard. Adapted dealers keep
their configured logos.
